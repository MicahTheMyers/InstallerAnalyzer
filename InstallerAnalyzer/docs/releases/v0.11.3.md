# Installer Analyzer v0.11.3

## Bootstrapper lifecycle reliability

- Replaces the short fixed observation window for unknown/bootstrapper installers with an activity-aware watcher that can observe for up to 15 minutes.
- Tracks the root installer, descendant processes, and relevant installer infrastructure such as `msiexec`, Adobe/Acrobat processes, and Microsoft Office Click-to-Run.
- Records PID/parent-PID relationships so bootstrapper telemetry is substantially less noisy and more useful for diagnosing child installers.
- Continuously checks uninstall-registry state while the bootstrapper is active instead of waiting until the outer process exits.
- Continuously captures newly staged EXE/MSI/MSIX/AppX/MSP/bundle files from common installer locations.
- Allows successful installed-application discovery even when the bootstrapper does not leave a child MSI behind (important for Office Click-to-Run).
- Distinguishes a bootstrapper timeout/still-active condition from a completed installer that produced no identifiable application.
- Preserves the validated MSI and MSIX lifecycle paths introduced in v0.11.1/v0.11.2.
