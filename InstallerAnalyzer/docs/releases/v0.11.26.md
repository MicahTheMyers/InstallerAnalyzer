# Installer Analyzer v0.11.26

## Lifecycle stage instrumentation

- Keeps the v0.11.25 Sandbox startup and host-side timeout foundation.
- Rewrites `install-analysis.checkpoint.json` at every lifecycle stage boundary.
- Adds `WorkflowStage`, `WorkflowStageUpdatedAt`, and `WorkflowStageHistory`.
- Host timeout now records the exact last checkpoint stage as `TimedOutStage`.
- Instruments uninstall candidate generation, pre-uninstall detection, initial silent uninstall, absence verification, silent strategy generation, each reinstall candidate, final uninstall, and final verification.
- No new watcher process or Sandbox session mechanism is introduced.
